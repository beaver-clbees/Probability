    Worksheet 6 Independent Events (1.4)
    

    Two events \(A\) and \(B\) are said to be events if the outcome of one does not change the probability of occurrence of the other.
    🔗

    1.

    Examples \(\&\) Non-examples
    🔗

    (a)

    Suppose you toss a coin twice. Let \(A\) be the event that the first toss is a head. Let \(B\) be the event that the second toss is a head. Are \(A\) and \(B\) independent?

    Suppose you draw two cards from a deck without replacement. Let \(A\) be the event that the first card is a Jack. Let \(B\) be the event that the second card is a Jack. Are \(A\) and \(B\) independent events?
