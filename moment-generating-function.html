    Definition 11.1. Moment Generating Function (B:2.3-1).

    Let \(X\) be a discrete R.V. with space \(S\) and pmf \(f(x)\text{.}\) If there exists a positive number \(h\) such that
    \begin{equation*} E(e^{tX}) = \sum_{x\in S}e^{tx}f(x) \end{equation*}
    exists and is finite for \(-h \lt t \lt h\) (interval around zero), then \(M(t) = E(e^{tX})\) is called the moment generating function of \(X\) (mgf).
    🔗
    🔗
    Suppose your sample space is \(\{x_{1}, x_{2}, \dots, x_{k}\}\text{.}\) Then
    \begin{equation*} M(t) = e^{tx_1}f(x_{1}) + e^{tx_2}f(x_{2}) + ... + e^{tx_k}f(x_{k}). \end{equation*}
