    5.

    During a blackout \(100\) people are arrested on suspicion of looting. Each person takes a polygraph test. Suppose it is known that the results of the polygraph are \(90\%\) reliable when administered to a guilty person and \(98\%\) reliable when given to an innocent person. Further suppose we know that the probability that any given suspect is guilty is \(0.12\text{.}\) A random suspect is tested and the polygraph result says that the person is guilty. Find the probability that the suspect is actually innocent.

Of the days that started out sunny, it rained for more than 3 hours during the day on 50 of those days. Suppose we choose one of the last 365 days at random and find that it rained more than 3 hours that day. What is the conditional probability that the day started out sunny?
